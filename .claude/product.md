## What Is This Product?

RiskGuard is a Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) SaaS platform built for UK tech startups. It gives growing engineering teams a single place to manage risk registers, map controls to compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, PCI DSS, GDPR, HIPAA), store audit evidence with SHA-256 integrity verification, and generate board-ready PDF reports — all without needing a dedicated compliance team. Built by TrusTrak Solutions Ltd, London.

## Primary Use Cases

1. **Audit prep** — A startup CTO uploads evidence, maps controls to ISO 27001, and generates a PDF report for an enterprise customer's security review — in weeks, not months.

2. **Ongoing risk management** — The engineering team tracks live risks, assigns owners, and monitors remediation progress through a single dashboard without spreadsheets.

3. **External audit** — An auditor is granted a time-limited access token that scopes them to relevant evidence only — no full account access, no oversharing.

---

## What Makes This Different?

- Built specifically for UK tech startups — not ported from enterprise tooling built for full compliance teams
- No compliance team required — sensible defaults, automation-first, plain-English AI guidance
- Tamper-evident evidence storage (SHA-256) — real audit trails, not checkbox theatre
- Multi-framework: one set of controls maps to all supported standards simultaneously

---

## What This Product Is NOT

- Not a document management system — it is a purpose-built GRC platform
- Not a legal or consulting service — it automates process, not legal judgement
- Not built for large enterprises with dedicated compliance departments
- Not a general-purpose project management tool

---

## Regulatory & Compliance Context

- RiskGuard itself handles customer compliance data — UK GDPR applies to all data processing
- Evidence files must be tamper-evident — SHA-256 integrity verification is a core product promise, not a nice-to-have
- Data must be stored in the UK / EU
- The product will eventually require its own ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certification

---

## Success Metrics

- Users achieve audit-readiness within 4 weeks of onboarding
- Controls map across all supported frameworks with zero manual duplication
- Zero evidence integrity failures (SHA-256 mismatch events)
- Enterprise deals not lost due to compliance delays
